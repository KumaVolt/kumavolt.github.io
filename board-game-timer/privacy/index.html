Privacy Policy — Board Game Timer

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Board Game Timer mobile app ("the App"), published by Kumavolt ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • The App is a turn timer for board games. It has no account and no login.
  • Anything you type in the App (like player names) stays on your device and is never sent to us.
  • We use Google AdMob to show a banner ad and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. These Google services collect limited device and usage data as described below.
  • We do not sell your data.

Information handled on your device only

Player names, colors, turn length and other settings are stored locally on your device (in the App's local storage). This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and is removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Information collected by third-party services

To keep the App free and to improve it, we use the following Google services, which may collect data automatically:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may process a device advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. See How Google uses information for ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics): may collect app events and usage, approximate region, device model, operating system version, and engagement time to help us understand and improve the App.

This data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Data transmitted by these services is encrypted in transit.

Consent and choices

  • Where required (e.g. in the EEA/UK), the App shows a consent form before serving personalized ads. You can change your ad-consent choice at any time from within the App.
  • On iOS, the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission; you may decline.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • You can turn the App's sound on or off in Settings (this is a device-side preference only).

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will address it.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because the App has no account, most data is either stored only on your device (which you can clear at any time) or held by Google as a processor. To exercise rights relating to Google-held data, use Google's privacy controls linked above, or contact us and we will assist.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
